Brainstorming Blueprint & Blue Ocean Market Playbook

01 Core Vision: Wearable Algorithmic DNA

Instead of competing in the saturated "graphic tee" market, we are creating a new category: Code-Driven Custom Darkwear.

The Core Hook: "What does your name look like as a mathematical equation?"
Customers enter their name, handle, or quote, select a shader theme (FLESH, GLITCHGEOM, VOIDDRIVE), and order a 1-of-1 printed t-shirt based on their exact mathematical fingerprint.

02 The Blue Ocean Market Advantage

Why this beats traditional clothing brands:

🔴 Traditional Red Ocean

Static PNG images, competing purely on price, hype, and high inventory costs.

🟢 Generative Blue Ocean

Interactive WebGL customization. 1-of-1 unique seeds. High perceived value and zero inventory risk (Print-On-Demand 2.0).

04 Apparel Design & Mockup Review

After testing multiple formats (All-Over Print, Large Rectangle, 1:1 Box Crop), the Centered 1:1 Chest Box Crop on a relaxed black tee emerged as the clear winner.

Why the 1:1 Box Works:

  • Rigid vs. Organic Contrast: The sharp geometric box frames the fluid fractal chaos.
  • Everyday Wearability: Fits seamlessly into high-end streetwear (Rick Owens, Cav Empt, Supreme style).
  • Optimal Print Quality: Clean screen/DTG print execution on 240+ GSM heavyweight cotton.

05 University & Guerrilla Launch Playbook

A 4-phase rollout strategy to build organic campus hype and word-of-mouth validation:

  • Phase 1 — Curiosity Marketing (Wear the Prototype): Wear the shirt on campus. When complimented, hand people your phone to let them play with the live WebGL shader customizer.
  • Phase 2 — QR / NFC Sleeves & Stickers: Include a subtle QR tag on the sleeve or hem that opens the web customizer directly. Hand out die-cut stickers.
  • Phase 3 — Micro-Influencer Seeding: Gift 3–5 custom-seeded shirts to key leaders in CS/Engineering clubs, campus DJs/rave organizers, and fashion/design students.
  • Phase 4 — Live Interactive Pop-Up: Connect a laptop to a projector/large screen at campus events where students manipulate the shader live and order their seed.

06 Technical Implementation Roadmap

  • Step 1: Integrate Name-to-Seed converter into index.html WebGL controls.
  • Step 2: Implement 4K offscreen canvas rendering (`3000x3000px`) for high-resolution print exports.
  • Step 3: Build instant mockup preview modal inside the web app.
  • Step 4: Connect print fulfillment API (Printful / Prodigi) for automated print-on-demand orders.
