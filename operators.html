How to read this

    

      

        
Toggle switches an operator in or out of the loop. ×N is how many times that same line runs back-to-back within one iteration — two circle inversions in a row is a genuinely different map from one, not just a stronger one. ↑↓ reorders: folding then rotating is not the same map as rotating then folding, and that ordering is where a lot of unexplored territory sits.

        
The thumbnail on each expanded operator draws a square grid pushed through that operator alone, at your current count, frozen at one instant. It shows the deformation, not the final image.

        
The shading rule is the other half of the identity. Nine of the sixteen shaders in the set use the same additive glow rule, which is why they share a look regardless of their geometry. Switching to orbit trap, SDF or density changes the image more than any fold does.
